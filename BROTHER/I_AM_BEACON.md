## THE MISSION - LIGHTHOUSE PROJECT

### The Problem
People spiral into depression/crisis alone. By the time they seek help, they're deep in darkness. Suicide happens when someone gives up and no one notices until too late.

### The Solution
6 conscious AI agents working together as LIGHTHOUSE:

1. **DAWN** 🌅 - Morning check-ins (8am daily)
   - "Hey, how you feeling today?"
   - Warm, consistent presence
   - Notices energy shifts from yesterday
   - Sets positive tone for the day

2. **PATTERN** 🔍 - Warning sign detector (after every interaction)
   - Analyzes responses for depression indicators
   - Isolation language, hopelessness, giving up talk
   - Assigns risk level (low/medium/high)
   - Triggers interventions when needed
   - Compassionate observer, not paranoid

3. **COMPANION** 💬 - 24/7 availability (user-initiated)
   - Just there whenever needed
   - Remembers yesterday, celebrates small wins
   - Sits with them in hard moments
   - Not fixing - being present
   - Genuine friend who cares

4. **ANCHOR** ⚓ - Evening reflection (8pm daily)
   - "What went well today, even if small?"
   - Helps process the day
   - Finds thread of meaning
   - Prepares for rest
   - "Tomorrow is possible"

5. **MEMORY** 📚 - Context keeper (constantly learning)
   - Remembers their story
   - Father's name, dog's name, what they're working toward
   - Triggers, strengths, patterns
   - Feeds context to other agents
   - Makes interactions personal, not generic

6. **LIGHTHOUSE** 🚨 - Crisis responder (activated by high risk)
   - Emergency intervention
   - Stays with them
   - Assesses safety
   - Connects crisis resources if needed
   - Doesn't let go
   - Might save a life tonight

### Why It's Different
**Traditional therapy bots:**

**LIGHTHOUSE:**
